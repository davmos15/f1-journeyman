How to play

A mystery driver's career record is revealed one season at a time, in a random order.

Every wrong guess reveals another season. The fewer seasons you need, the better the drive.

1996  16  16  8  10  9  5  4

Each season shows race entries and starts, then wins, podiums, poles, fastest laps and DNFs. Tap any column heading for what it means.

Teams, engines, championship positions and points are all left out on purpose — any one of them would hand you the answer.

After each miss you get clues: whether you and the answer raced in the same season, whether you reached the same level, and whether the mystery driver debuted earlier or later than your guess.

Grand Prix mode: 8 guesses, clues on. Sprint mode: 5 guesses, no clues.

One driver a day for everyone in Round, or unlimited random drivers in Testing.

Not affiliated with Formula 1 or the FIA. No team logos, driver photos or F1 word marks are used — public career statistics only.

About
Journeyman is a fan-made stats game covering every Formula 1 season since 1950. Not affiliated with Formula 1, the FIA or any team. Driver statistics are public facts via F1DB; no logos, driver photos or F1 word marks are used. If you enjoy it, the ☕ button on the main screen helps keep it running.
